Lab 2: City Multivariate Visualization

Temperature, development level, population, and region.

Design Justification

I mapped temperature to horizontal position because position makes numerical values easy to compare. Development level is shown on the vertical axis because Low, Medium, and High have a clear order. Population is mapped to circle size, so larger cities appear more prominent. Region is mapped to color because it is categorical and has no natural order. This design separates cities by development level while keeping temperature comparisons clear. It also allows viewers to quickly notice larger cities, regional patterns, and possible differences in temperature across development levels. Tooltips provide the exact values for each city without adding too much text to the chart.
