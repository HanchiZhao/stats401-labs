Short Analysis

I used the Twitter Financial News Topic dataset, which contains finance-related tweets categorized into 20 topics. I analyzed the validation split, which initially contained 4,117 tweets. During cleaning, I checked missing tweet text, duplicate records, malformed strings, and topic labels. No missing text, duplicate records, or invalid topic labels were found, so all 4,117 tweets remained after cleaning. Sentiment was estimated using the CardiffNLP Twitter RoBERTa model, and the numeric sentiment score was calculated as the probability of positive sentiment minus the probability of negative sentiment. The visualization maps financial topic to vertical position and average sentiment score to horizontal position. Legal | Regulation had the lowest average score at -0.1908, while Stock Commentary had the highest average score at 0.2606. These values are model-generated estimates rather than ground-truth sentiment labels.
